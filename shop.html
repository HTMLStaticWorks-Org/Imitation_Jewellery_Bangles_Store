Size & fit guide

Find your perfect bangle size

Ensure your bangles slide on smoothly and stay securely on your wrist with our standardized Indian sizing chart.

Bangle Size Inner Diameter (Inches) Inner Diameter (mm) Hand Circumference (Inches) Fit Recommendation
2.4 (Small) 2.25 in 57.2 mm 7.06 in Petite / Slim wrists
2.6 (Medium) 2.37 in 60.3 mm 7.45 in Most popular standard size

💡 How to measure: Bring your thumb and little finger together as if putting on a bangle, wrap a string around the widest part of your hand, and measure the string against a ruler.
