Our story & heritage

A little sparkle, a lot of heart

Lustre Lane was founded with a singular conviction: luxury-grade Indian jewellery should be accessible, comfortable and empowering for every occasion, without the fragile worries of traditional gold.

✦ Founded in Chennai ❖ 100+ Heritage Karigars 🌱 Ethical & Recycled Brass ✨ 50,000+ Celebrations Adorned

Style should feel like you.

We believe jewellery is more than an accessory. It is a memory, a mood and a small way to express who you are. Our collection brings together contemporary designs inspired by timeless Indian craftsmanship.

From a single everyday bangle to a full celebration bridal look, every piece is sculpted to help you feel confident, radiant and beautifully yourself.

We work directly with generational artisan clusters across Jaipur, Rajkot and Tamil Nadu, eliminating unnecessary middlemen to deliver authentic karigari directly to your door.

Behind the scenes

Our 4-stage ethical crafting process

How an idea transforms into a radiant piece ready to be cherished for years.

1

Hand-Drawn Sketches

Concepts drawn by our in-house designers blending historic architectural motifs with modern weight-distribution.

2

Eco-Brass Mold Forging

Molds are carved by master artisans from recycled, lead-free brass to guarantee durability and sharp crispness.

3

24K Micron Gold Plating

Triple-layer electroplating baths impart a genuine 24-karat golden sheen that resists tarnishing and dulling.

4

Stone Setting & Polish

Faux polki and kundan stones are set by hand, followed by a hypoallergenic protective seal for total skin comfort.
