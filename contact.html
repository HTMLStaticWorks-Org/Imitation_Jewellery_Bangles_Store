Everything you need to know

Quick answers regarding sizing, skin safety, shipping, custom styling and exchanges.

We stock four standard Indian sizes: 2.4 (Small - 57.2mm), 2.6 (Medium - 60.3mm), 2.8 (Large - 63.5mm), and 2.10 (Extra Large - 66.7mm). To measure, bring your thumb and pinky finger together as if putting on a bangle, wrap a piece of string around the widest part of your hand, and measure the string against a ruler. Our size 2.6 fits over 70% of customers comfortably.

Never. All Lustre Lane jewellery is constructed with recycled brass cores dipped in a triple-layer 24K micron electroplating bath and finished with a clear hypoallergenic resin seal. They are 100% free of nickel, cadmium and lead, making them safe for sensitive skin types.

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours from our Chennai facility. Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days for metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata) and 3 to 6 business days for other regional pins. All orders over ₹1,499 receive free insured express shipping.

Our stylists will hand-curate a custom glass, kada and stone stack tailored to your outfit within 4 hours.

We offer a hassle-free 7-day exchange window from the date of delivery. If a bangle size does not fit or if you prefer an alternate finish, our team will arrange a doorstep exchange at no extra cost.
